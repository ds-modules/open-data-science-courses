# Lecture Slides and Demos

Data 9 lecture materials are organized as Jupyter notebooks-usable as slide decks in class and as runnable demos for students.

## Where to find them

- **[Lecture folder on GitHub](https://github.com/arianag27/CS-9/tree/main/content/lecture)** - notebooks grouped by topic (intro, pandas, visualization, modeling, text wrangling, and more).
- **[Course schedule (JupyterLite)](https://arianag27.github.io/CS-9/)** - the home page links each week to the matching lecture notebook. Use **Lecture** for slide decks and demos; use **Textbook** for assigned reading.

## Topics covered

Lecture notebooks align with the textbook and weekly schedule, including:

- Introduction and Data 8 review
- Pandas I, II, and III
- Text wrangling and regular expressions
- Visualization I and II
- Sampling
- Modeling (simple linear regression through decision trees and random forests)
- SQL I and II

To run a notebook locally or on your campus JupyterHub, clone the [CS-9 repository](https://github.com/arianag27/CS-9) and open files under `content/lecture/`.
